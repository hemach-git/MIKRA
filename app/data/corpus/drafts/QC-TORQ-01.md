## 1. Torque Specifications
Bolt torque specifications: M6 bolts 10 Nm. M8 bolts 25 Nm. M10 bolts 52 Nm. M12 bolts 85 Nm. Tolerance is plus or minus 8 percent. DRAFT pending approval.

## 2. Torque Verification
1. Verify bolt torque on the first 5 parts of every shift with a calibrated torque wrench.
2. Then audit 1 part per hour.
3. Record results on the torque check sheet.
