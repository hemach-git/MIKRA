## 1. Spindle Overheating (Alarm 2041)
Alarm 2041 indicates CNC-04 spindle temperature above 65 C.
1. Stop the program and let the spindle run at idle 500 rpm for 5 minutes to cool.
2. Check spindle chiller coolant level and fan.
3. Clean the chiller air filter.
4. If the alarm returns within one shift, stop the machine and call the maintenance lead; bearing wear is likely.

## 2. Coolant System
CNC-04 coolant concentration must be 6-8 percent measured with the refractometer. Top up with premix only. Clean the chip conveyor and coolant tank screen weekly.

## 3. Way Lubrication (Alarm 1102)
Alarm 1102 means low way-lube oil. Refill the lube reservoir with ISO VG 68 way oil and press reset.

## 4. Tool Changer Fault (Alarm 3310)
Alarm 3310 indicates the tool changer arm did not reach home. Put the machine in manual mode, apply lockout before entering the enclosure, and check for chips blocking the arm sensor.
