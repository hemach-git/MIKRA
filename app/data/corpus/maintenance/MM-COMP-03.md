## 1. Low Air Pressure
Plant air pressure should be 6.5-7.0 bar. If air pressure drops:
1. Check for air leaks at quick couplings and hoses (listen for hissing).
2. Check the compressor inlet air filter; replace it if the restriction indicator is red.
3. Check the dryer and drain valve.
4. If pressure is below 5.5 bar, start backup compressor AC-1.

## 2. Condensate Drain
Drain the compressor receiver tank daily at shift start. Check the automatic drain valve weekly.

## 3. Filters and Oil
Replace the compressor inlet filter every 2,000 hours and compressor oil every 4,000 hours.
