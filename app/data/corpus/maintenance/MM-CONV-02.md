## 1. Overview
Conveyor C3 is a 24-meter belt conveyor feeding the packing line. Drive motor is 3 kW. Normal belt speed is 0.5 m/s.

## 2. Clearing a Belt Jam
Jams on Conveyor C3 usually occur at the transfer plate near the packing station.
1. Stop the conveyor and apply lockout per SOP-LOTO-04.
2. Open the access cover at the transfer plate.
3. Remove the jammed cartons by hand; use the hook tool for items under the plate.
4. Inspect the belt edge for damage and the transfer plate gap (must be 3-5 mm).
5. Close the cover, remove lockout and restart at low speed for one minute.

## 3. Belt Tracking and Tension
If the conveyor belt drifts to one side, adjust the tail pulley take-up screw a quarter turn on the side the belt is drifting toward. Correct belt tension allows 15-20 mm deflection at mid-span with hand pressure.

## 4. Motor Overload Trips
Repeated conveyor motor overload trips usually indicate a seized roller or over-tensioned belt. Check rollers for free rotation and reduce tension before resetting the overload relay.
