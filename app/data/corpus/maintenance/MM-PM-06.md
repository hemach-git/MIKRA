## 1. Daily PM Tasks
Conveyor C3 visual belt check. Compressor AC-2 tank drain. Press Line 2 oil level and stopping time check.

## 2. Weekly PM and Greasing
CNC-04 coolant concentration and tank screen cleaning. Grease Conveyor C3 drive bearings with lithium EP2 grease, 2 pumps per grease fitting.

## 3. Monthly PM Tasks
Press Line 2 hydraulic filter check. Robot Cell R7 cable dress inspection. Calibrate shop torque wrenches (quality lab).

## 4. PM Logging
Record every completed preventive maintenance task in the maintenance log with date, asset, technician and findings.
