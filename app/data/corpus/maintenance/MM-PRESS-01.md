## 1. Hydraulic Pressure Loss
Normal press working pressure is 180-200 bar.
1. Check hydraulic oil level in the sight glass.
2. Inspect hoses and fittings for leaks.
3. Check the pressure relief valve setting.
4. If pressure stays below 160 bar, stop production and call the maintenance lead.

## 2. Hydraulic Oil Leak
Small weeping at fittings: tighten the fitting after releasing pressure. Active leak or spray: stop immediately, apply lockout, and place absorbent. Never check for leaks with your hand; high-pressure oil can penetrate skin.

## 3. Die Change
1. Run the ram to the bottom position at inch speed.
2. Apply lockout and install the ram safety block.
3. Unclamp the die and roll it out on the die cart.
4. Install the new die, clamp to 40 Nm, remove the safety block and run one test stroke at inch speed.

## 4. Daily Checks
Check press oil temperature (below 55 C), oil level, and clutch/brake stopping time display (must be under 180 ms).
