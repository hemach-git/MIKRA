## 1. Weld Tip Dressing
Dress the robot spot weld tips every 250 welds or when weld quality alarms appear. Replace tips when length is below 12 mm.

## 2. Robot Fault Recovery
1. Read the robot fault code on the teach pendant.
2. Switch to manual mode (T1) before entering the cell.
3. Apply lockout if any part of your body enters the robot envelope.
4. Clear the cause, then reset the fault and run the robot to home position at reduced speed.

## 3. Wire Feed Problems
Welding wire bird-nesting is usually caused by worn drive rolls or a kinked liner. Replace drive rolls every 6 months.
