## 1. First Article Inspection
The first part after any setup, tool change or program change must be measured on the CMM before production continues.

## 2. Calipers and Micrometers
Zero calipers before each use. Check calipers against the gauge block at shift start. Measure each critical dimension 3 times and record the average.

## 3. CNC-04 Bore Tolerance
The CNC-04 main bore diameter is 25.000 mm with tolerance +0.021 / -0.000 mm. Measure the bore with the bore gauge every 10 parts.

## 4. Gauge Out of Calibration
If a gauge fails its check, tag it out, and re-inspect all parts measured since the last good check.
