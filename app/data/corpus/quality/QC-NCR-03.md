## 1. Identifying Nonconforming Material
Any part that does not meet the drawing, specification or visual standard is nonconforming material.

## 2. Hold Tag Procedure
1. Attach a red HOLD tag to the part or container.
2. Move it to the red quarantine area.
3. Open a Nonconformance Report (NCR) in the quality system with part number, quantity and defect.

## 3. Disposition of Held Parts
Only the Quality Engineer may disposition held material as use-as-is, rework, or scrap. Supervisors must not release held material.
