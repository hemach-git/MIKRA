## 1. Paint Film Thickness
Dry paint film thickness must be 60-80 microns, measured with the coating thickness gauge at 5 points per part.

## 2. Paint Visual Defects
Reject painted parts with runs, sags, bare spots or contamination larger than 1 mm on Class A surfaces. Minor orange peel is acceptable on Class B surfaces.

## 3. Common Paint Defect Causes
Orange peel: paint too thick or gun too far. Runs and sags: too much paint or low booth temperature. Fish eyes: oil or silicone contamination.

## 4. Paint Booth Conditions
Paint booth temperature 20-27 C, humidity 40-60 percent.
