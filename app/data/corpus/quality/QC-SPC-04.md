## 1. Control Chart Use
Operators plot X-bar and R control charts for critical dimensions every hour using a subgroup of 5 parts.

## 2. Out-of-Control Rules
The process is out of control if: one point is outside the control limits; 7 points in a row are on one side of the center line; or 6 points in a row steadily increase or decrease.

## 3. SPC Reaction Plan
1. Stop the process.
2. Notify the quality technician.
3. Check tool wear, material and setup.
4. Inspect parts made since the last in-control point.

## 4. Process Capability
Critical characteristics must have Cpk of at least 1.33.
