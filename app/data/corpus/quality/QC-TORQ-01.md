## 1. Torque Specifications
Bolt torque specifications: M6 bolts 10 Nm. M8 bolts 25 Nm. M10 bolts 45 Nm. M12 bolts 80 Nm. Tolerance is plus or minus 10 percent.

## 2. Torque Verification
1. Verify bolt torque on the first 5 parts of every shift with a calibrated torque wrench.
2. Then audit 1 part per hour.
3. Record results on the torque check sheet.

## 3. Torque Out of Specification
If a torque reading is outside tolerance, place all parts since the last good check on hold with a red hold tag and notify the quality technician.

## 4. Torque Tool Calibration
Torque tools are calibrated monthly. Do not use a torque wrench with an expired calibration sticker.
