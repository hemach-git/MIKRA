## 1. Weld Visual Inspection
Inspect 100 percent of welds visually. Welds must be continuous with no cracks.

## 2. Weld Acceptance Criteria
Weld porosity: no single pore larger than 1.5 mm. Undercut: maximum 0.5 mm deep. Spot weld nugget diameter at least 5 mm.

## 3. Weld Destructive Testing
One part per shift from Robot Cell R7 is peel-tested. Record nugget diameter on the weld test log.

## 4. Failed Weld
Place suspect welded parts on hold per QC-NCR-03 and notify the quality technician.
