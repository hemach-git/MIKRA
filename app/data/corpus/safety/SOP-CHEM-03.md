## 1. Safety Data Sheets
Every chemical on site must have a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) available at the area SDS station and in the online SDS library. Read the SDS before first use.

## 2. Storage
Flammable solvents and paint thinners are stored in the yellow flammable cabinet. Keep no more than one shift supply at Paint Booth 1. Keep containers closed when not in use.

## 3. Transfer and Labeling
1. Ground and bond metal containers before transferring flammable liquids.
2. Label every secondary container with product name and hazard pictogram.
3. Never mix chemicals unless the procedure says so.

## 4. Coolant and Oil
CNC-04 coolant concentrate is a skin irritant. Wear nitrile gloves when mixing coolant and wash hands before eating.
