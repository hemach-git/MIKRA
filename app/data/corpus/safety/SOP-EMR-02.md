## 1. Fire or Smoke
1. Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station.
2. Call plant emergency line ext. 5555.
3. Use a fire extinguisher only if the fire is small and you are trained.
4. Evacuate to the assembly point at the north parking lot and report to your supervisor for headcount.

## 2. Injury
1. Do not move a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.
2. Call ext. 5555 and request the first aid team.
3. Lock out the equipment involved and preserve the scene.
4. The supervisor completes the incident report within the same shift.

## 3. Chemical Spill
1. Alert people nearby and leave the immediate area if vapors are strong.
2. For small spills under 4 liters, trained staff use the yellow spill kit and nitrile gloves.
3. For large spills, evacuate and call ext. 5555.
4. Check the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for the chemical before cleanup.

## 4. Gas Leak
Evacuate the area immediately, do not operate electrical switches, and call ext. 5555 from a safe location.
