## 1. Operator Requirements
Only certified operators may drive forklifts. Certification is renewed every 3 years. Complete the pre-use inspection checklist each shift.

## 2. Speed and Pedestrians
Maximum forklift speed is 8 km/h on the production floor. Pedestrians use marked walkways. Sound the horn at intersections and blind corners.

## 3. Pre-Use Inspection
1. Check forks, chains and mast for damage.
2. Test brakes, horn and lights.
3. Check for hydraulic leaks.
4. If any check fails, tag the forklift out of service and report it.
