## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to any servicing, repair, jam clearing, cleaning or die change where a person could be exposed to unexpected startup or release of stored energy (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, gravity, thermal).
WARNING: Never reach into a machine, conveyor or press to clear a jam while it is energized.

## 2. Authorized Persons
Only trained authorized employees may apply locks. Each person working on the equipment applies their own personal red lock and tag. Supervisors verify training records before assigning lockout work.

## 3. Lockout Steps
1. Notify affected operators that the equipment will be shut down and locked out.
2. Stop the machine using the normal stop control.
3. Isolate all energy sources at the main disconnect, hydraulic valve and air supply valve.
4. Apply your personal lock and danger tag to each isolation point.
5. Release stored energy: bleed hydraulic and air pressure, block raised rams or dies, let hot surfaces cool.
6. Verify zero energy by pressing the start button and checking gauges read zero (tryout).
7. Perform the work only after zero energy is verified.

## 4. Removing Locks and Restart
1. Remove tools and confirm guards are reinstalled.
2. Confirm all persons are clear of the danger zone.
3. Each person removes only their own lock.
4. Notify affected operators before restart.
WARNING: Never remove another person's lock. Lost-key removal requires EHS Manager approval.
