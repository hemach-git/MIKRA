## 1. Guarding Rule
All guards, interlocked doors and light curtains must be in place and working before production. Operating with a missing or bypassed guard is prohibited.
WARNING: Never bypass, tape over or defeat a light curtain or door interlock.

## 2. Daily Light Curtain Check
1. At shift start, pass the test rod through the light curtain at top, middle and bottom.
2. The press must stop each time.
3. If the press does not stop, tag the press out of service and call maintenance.

## 3. Damaged Guard
If a guard is damaged or missing, stop the machine, apply lockout, and report it to the supervisor. Production may restart only after maintenance repairs the guard.
