## 1. General PPE
Safety glasses with side shields and safety-toe shoes are required everywhere on the production floor. Hearing protection is required where posted (above 85 dBA).

## 2. Press and Machining Areas
Press Line 2 and CNC-04 require safety glasses, hearing protection and cut-resistant gloves (level A4) when handling sheet metal or chips. Never wear gloves near rotating spindles.

## 3. Paint Booth and Chemical Areas
Paint Booth 1 requires an organic-vapor half-face respirator, nitrile gloves, and a disposable coverall. Respirator fit test must be current (within 12 months).

## 4. Welding Areas
Robot Cell R7 and the Weld Shop require an auto-darkening welding helmet (shade 10-13) when inside the cell, a flame-resistant jacket and leather welding gloves.
WARNING: Never enter Robot Cell R7 with the robot in automatic mode.
